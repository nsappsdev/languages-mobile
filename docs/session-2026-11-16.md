# Session record — 16 November 2026

This record continues [the earlier session notes](session-2026-11-11.md).

## New reader behavior

- Accept sentence-only manifest version 3 alongside existing version 2 lessons. Each sentence plays from its own recording while progress, seeking, and word highlighting use the lesson timeline.
- Repeat learning words/phrases from their original sentence occurrence. This preserves the same voice, tone, and pronunciation rather than switching to an independently generated vocabulary voice.
- Prepare the current sentence on opening the lesson and keep two sentences ahead. Later downloads do not block the first prepared sentence from playing.
- Retain prepared segments and loaded mode queues for quick mode changes. Repetitions and pauses are built into each sentence file; native playlists move between sentences.
- Pause/resume retains the exact practice position, including pauses and sentence replays. New seeks supersede older pending preparation, failed downloads can be retried without skipping a sentence, and leaving the reader releases its players and temporary files.
- Persist downloaded audio in browser Cache Storage as well as the existing private native cache. Account/server/asset/content identity prevents stale recordings while allowing unchanged audio to survive republishing. Logout clears the cache; bounded caches evict older unused entries.
- Avoid unnecessary reloads of a ready native playlist item and retain final progress when the native player clears its completed item.

Details: [sentence-only playback](sentence-first-playback.md) and [startup and cache design](sentence-playback-startup.md).

- The local “14 law” reader was tested for Introduction, Teaching, Deep Learning, word repetitions, sentence replays, pause/resume, restart, and seeking. Audio cache hits after a page reload confirmed persistent source reuse.
- Observed local browser startup was approximately 0.27–0.64 seconds across the recorded checks. Diagnostics measure time from the handler to the first observed playback-clock advance, not physical speaker output. These development-browser results are not Android release benchmarks.

- Native device timing remains unverified because no Android/iOS device was available. No new Expo build, store submission, or deployment is included in this commit-and-push finalization.
